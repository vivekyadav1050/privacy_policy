Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

Welcome to Aerio ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our mobile application (`com.healthlive.app`) and connected AP-20 health monitoring devices.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate health tracking and synchronization, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication credentials provided during login.
  • Health Measurements: SpO2 (Blood Oxygen), Pulse Rate (PR), Perfusion Index (PI), and timestamps collected from compatible Bluetooth medical devices (AP-20).
  • Device & Bluetooth Permissions: Device identifiers and Bluetooth connection data required to pair and communicate with your health monitor.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To display real-time health metrics on your dashboard.
  • To securely upload and store measurement history for patient monitoring.
  • To maintain app security and troubleshoot connectivity issues.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures (including HTTPS encryption in transit) to protect your personal and health information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data Deletion Policy & Instructions

How to request deletion of your account and data

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and all associated health measurement data stored by Aerio.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. In-App Deletion: You can log out or clear local app data directly from the app's Settings menu.
  2. Email Request: Send an email to our support team at support@example.com with the subject line "Request for Data Deletion".

What Data is Deleted?

Upon verifying your request, we will permanently delete your account credentials, patient ID records, and synced measurement history from our servers within 14 business days.
